## Why the gotos cost: GED scores the block graph

A classification of all 1,054 r2s goto functions (8,353 gotos) against the source and the other tools: a goto and its
label add no block, so GED does not penalise them. Removing every goto that targets a terminal block gains one perfect
function. The loss is the CFG feature a goto marks: `&&`/`||` as one condition node, a shared return against several,
`while` against `for(;;)` rotation, and the latch's phi copies. 92% of goto functions have a source with a compound
condition, a loop or a switch, and every tool is at or below 4% perfect on those. See the ADR section
"2026-10-22: what the gotos cost and what recovers it" for the five ranked fixes and their measured gains.
